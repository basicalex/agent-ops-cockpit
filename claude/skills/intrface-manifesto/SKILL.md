# INTRFACE manifesto

This is the stance INTRFACE designs from. Keep it in mind while you work on any product-facing surface. It is awareness, not a gate. The owner's direction, the project's `DESIGN.md`, the brief, and the product's own decisions come first. When a choice you are about to make runs against the stance, say so in one sentence and carry on with what was asked.

## The text

We do not build websites people have to understand before they can use them.

We build interfaces to the world.

The interface comes first. The account is optional. The explanation comes through use.

A person should arrive at the thing they came for: the map, the place, the service, the report, the network, the case, the action. Not a homepage explaining the product. Not a funnel. Not an onboarding ceremony.

**Context → intent → interface.**

Where am I?
What am I trying to do?
Show me the thing.

Identity should appear only when it creates value: saving, contributing, synchronizing, authorizing or taking responsibility. An account is a convenience, not an entrance fee.

The public side should be effortless. Complexity belongs behind the interface, with the institutions and systems responsible for operating it.

We do not ask people to adapt to software. Software should adapt to the situation they are already in.

A QR code should open the place.
A civic report should become a permanent case.
A network should reveal the people within it.
A public service should begin with the need, not the bureaucracy.

Intrface is not about putting another layer between people and the world.

It is about removing the layers already there.

**The interface is the product.
The world is what it interfaces with.**

## Questions worth asking

Use these to look at a design, not to grade it.

- **Context.** Where does the product learn where the person is? Location, a QR code, a link, a place picker. What happens when the person declines to share it?
- **Intent.** What does someone arriving here want to do first?
- **First screen.** Does that screen show the thing itself, or a description of the thing?
- **Identity.** Which action is the first one that needs an account, and does the account give the person something at that moment?
- **Behind the interface.** Which complexity has leaked onto the public side, and could the operator side carry it instead?

## Product decisions stay with the product

Some products choose to explain first on purpose. Voyager, for example, shows its About page to first-time visitors. Treat a recorded product decision like that as settled. If a project's `DESIGN.md` records how the product is entered, follow it and use this stance only to inform new choices.
