## Scope and method

This standalone synthesis reconciles three bounded passes across physical pp. 1–49, 50–97, and 98–144. It covers the Impeccable [entrypoint](../../impeccable/SKILL.md) plus 21 topic references:

- Composition: [layout](../../impeccable/reference/layout.md), [distill](../../impeccable/reference/distill.md), [shape](../../impeccable/reference/shape.md), [new-work](../../impeccable/reference/new-work.md), [critique](../../impeccable/reference/critique.md).
- Craft: [typeset](../../impeccable/reference/typeset.md), [colorize](../../impeccable/reference/colorize.md), [craft-floor](../../impeccable/reference/craft-floor.md), [bolder](../../impeccable/reference/bolder.md), [quieter](../../impeccable/reference/quieter.md), [polish](../../impeccable/reference/polish.md), [overdrive](../../impeccable/reference/overdrive.md).
- Interaction: [operate](../../impeccable/reference/operate.md), [clarify](../../impeccable/reference/clarify.md), [harden](../../impeccable/reference/harden.md), [adapt](../../impeccable/reference/adapt.md), [animate](../../impeccable/reference/animate.md), [delight](../../impeccable/reference/delight.md), [onboard](../../impeccable/reference/onboard.md), [audit](../../impeccable/reference/audit.md), [optimize](../../impeccable/reference/optimize.md).

The 2026-09-24 re-audit checked every citation against Impeccable 4.3.1. Each passage cited from 4.0.4 still exists; citations now name the file and the nearest heading instead of line numbers, which shift between releases. It also checked the material added since 4.0.4: comp-led and code-led builds, capture validity, and verdict scope in [new-work](../../impeccable/reference/new-work.md); comp discipline in [visualize](../../impeccable/reference/visualize.md); the browser-surfaces and geometric-mask bullets in craft-floor; the reduced-motion path in animate; the degraded finish-reviewer, documenter, and asset-producer agents. None of it changes a Status. D4 and D20 now cite the new material and state a narrower companion delta.

`Covered` means Impeccable already carries the substance. `Partial` means this companion adds a focused procedure, not a claim that Impeccable lacks the principle. `Conflict` marks blanket or fixed advice that should stay contextual. `New` marks a bounded operational refinement in this companion. Synthesis beyond the book is named as such.

No browser, live site, user test, analytics review, or source-site defect audit formed part of this comparison. It establishes no defect in any product and no user outcome. The PDF and its excerpts are not shipped with this skill.

## Excluded blanket advice

- Decorative cards, mandatory shadow tiers, blur, glass, gradients, dark mode, and fixed border recipes are not general upgrades.
- Equal card height, equal copy length, and universal image crops must not distort facts or intrinsic media.
- Typeface choice must not follow an industry stereotype.
- Fixed 16px, line-height ratios, character ranges, three-click rules, four-choice limits, and button-order recipes are heuristics at most, not standards.
- More interaction does not imply more motion. Two-column layouts, overlap, bottom navigation, and one-click purchase remain contextual.

## Companion boundary

The companion adds decision procedures, exceptions, and evidence labels. It does not fork or replace Impeccable, relax project rules, or grant permission to edit, browse, upload, submit, purchase, or run destructive checks. No source-site or browser defects were established by this audit.
